# margin 🌿

give yourself margin.

margin is a study planner for university students that breaks assignments into time-boxed tasks, slots them into your real available hours, and gently checks in with you at the end of each day.

## why margin?

most planners assume infinite time. margin starts with the time you actually have — your real weekly availability — and fits your work into it. no streaks, no gamification, no nagging. just a quiet, honest tool for managing your week.

## features (v1)

- 📅 set your recurring weekly availability once, edit per term
- 📚 organize your work as courses → projects → tasks
- 📄 upload an assignment PDF, get an AI-generated task breakdown to review and approve
- 🎯 drag tasks onto your availability blocks to plan your week
- 🌙 end-of-day check-in: mark what's done, reschedule what isn't

## stack

- **frontend:** vanilla JS, mobile-first PWA
- **backend:** Flask (deployed on Render or Railway)
- **database:** Firestore
- **auth:** Firebase Google Auth
- **ai:** Anthropic Claude API (for PDF → tasks)
- **storage:** Firebase Storage (for PDF uploads)
